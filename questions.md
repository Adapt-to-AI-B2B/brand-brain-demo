# Questions to ask the Este company brain

Open Claude Code in this folder and ask any of these in plain words. Ask Claude to show the
query it used, then check one number you can verify yourself.

"Today" is the latest date in the data (2026-09-30 until new days are loaded).

## Look something up
- **L1** How many Men's T-shirts (all sizes and colours) did we sell in September 2026, stores and online together?
- **L2** How many Men's T-shirts in Medium / Green does Lisboa Chiado have in stock today?
- **L3** Who makes the Men's T-shirt, and what is their lead time?
- **L4** What does the Women's Crewneck cost in the UK (GBP)?
- **L5** How many purchase orders are still open, and for how many units in total?
- **L6** For the AW26 season, how many Men's T-shirts are planned, and how many have been produced so far?

## Compare
- **C1** Which store sold the most units in September 2026?
- **C2** In September 2026, across all products, did stores or online sell more units?
- **C3** What are our five best-selling styles over the last 12 months?
- **C4** Which country buys the most from us online?
- **C5** Lisboa stores or Porto stores: who sold more units over the last 12 months?
- **C6** Which size of the Men's T-shirt sells best?

## See a trend
- **T1** How many Men's T-shirts did we sell in each of the last 8 weeks?
- **T2** Which month of the last 12 had the highest sales in units?
- **T3** How did the Hoodie (style code HOO) sell month by month?
- **T4** How many online orders did we get each month? Do November and December stand out?
- **T5** What share of online sales came back as returns over the last 12 months?

## Check that the systems agree
- **R1** Does Shopify's stock match Sage's stock for the online warehouse? Where not?
- **R2** For any difference you found: why is it there?
- **R3** Do the product room (Notion) and the production plan agree on who supplies each style?

## What if
- **W1** How many weeks of stock do we have left of the Men's T-shirt at the current sales speed?
- **W2** If we plan 8,000 AW26 Men's T-shirts instead of 7,000, how much more cash do the extra 1,000 tie up, and how many more weeks of stock do they give us at today's sales speed?
- **W3** If we reorder the Men's T-shirt today, when would it arrive?
- **W4** Which five products will run out first at the current sales speed?
- **W5** How much of the new AW26 collection have we sold so far (sell-through)?
